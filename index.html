📜 The Mimiac North Star Manifesto

Why Mimiac exists: Category of One

Mimiac is not a digital textbook. It is a pocket voice-acting studio for language shadowing.

1. The Broken Status Quo

Modern language apps train your thumb to tap silent multiple-choice boxes. That teaches passive reading, leaving learners deaf to natural speed and afraid to speak out loud.

2. Why Drama Unlocks Fluency

Accents live in prosody—sudden pitch spikes, sarcastic drops, and bursts of speed. Acting out high-stakes dialogue removes self-consciousness and trains your mouth twice as fast.

3. Meet Your Mimiac

A Mimiac is a minimalist reactive creature living inside the dot of mimi.ac. It doesn’t lecture you with grammar rules—it mirrors your mood, leaning in when you listen and pulsing with your mic.

4. What We Refuse to Build

  • No dry conjugation tables or silent tap-the-word drills.
  • No flat, monotone robot voices draining emotion out of speech.
  • No bloated menus—just Stage, Drama Packs, and your Mimiac.

Questions about Mimiac (mimi.ac)

What is Mimiac and what does mimi.ac mean?

Mimiac (from mimic + maniac) is a playful language shadowing and accent coaching app. Our domain mimi.ac spells out our name directly—just like youtu.be spells YouTube—and powers short, shareable challenge links for your shadowing clips.

How is Mimiac different from Duolingo or ELSA Speak?

Duolingo focuses on reading and vocabulary streaks; traditional pronunciation apps rely on dry drills. Mimiac is built entirely around emotional language shadowing—short soap-opera scenes with expressive voice acting and AI feedback on both pronunciation and dramatic delivery.

Which languages will Mimiac support at launch?

Our Founding Beta launches with Drama Packs in Spanish, French, Japanese, Italian, and English, with new episodic packs rolling out continuously based on beta tester votes.
